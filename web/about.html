About Shape-Shift Racer

High-Octane 2.5D Synthwave Arcade Racing with Real-Time Vehicle Morphing

Shape-Shift Racer by Sarthak is an indie retro-futuristic arcade racer created with pure client-side web technologies. Combining the vibrant aesthetic of 1980s synthwave with modern browser capabilities, the game challenges players to navigate endless neon highways, morph between vehicle forms at blazing speeds, dodge barricades, and build massive streak multipliers.

Core Game Mechanics

Real-Time Vehicle Morphing Engine

Unlike traditional racing games where vehicle dimensions remain static, Shape-Shift Racer requires dynamic shape-shifting to survive highway hazards:

  • CRUISER (Normal): 84 × 50 px balanced sports GT coupe for standard road cruising and navigating lateral lane shifts.
  • LOW-RIDER (Flat): 136 × 22 px ultra-low aerodynamic supercar designed to slide beneath laser low beams (30 px clearance gap).
  • SLIM-BLADE (Narrow): 32 × 100 px razor-thin concept racer engineered to thread through narrow twin pillar gates (48 px gap width).

Dynamic Biomes & Speed Scaling

The race progresses across four atmospheric visual biomes transitioning smoothly every 500 meters:

  • Sunset Drive: Golden horizon, deep magenta skies, and violet asphalt curbs.
  • Cyber Night: Deep cobalt grid, electric cyan lighting, and neon signposts.
  • Neon Tokyo: Glowing emerald highway with vibrant hot-pink accents.
  • Plasma Storm: Fiery crimson storm skies, molten amber glow, and maximum velocity.

Speed scales dynamically from an initial cruise speed of 420 km/h up to a supersonic 920 km/h, challenging your reflexes and morph timings as your streak grows from 1x to 5x.

Technical Architecture

Shape-Shift Racer is built with strict performance and privacy principles:

  • Pure Canvas 2D Pseudo-3D Projection: The 3D road rendering is generated using mathematical perspective projection (p = raw_p^2.2) with dynamic sinusoidal curvature and segment shading, achieving a locked 60 FPS without external 3D engine overhead.
  • Procedural Web Audio Engine: All sound effects (morphing chimes, obstacle clears, coin sparkles, crash impacts) and the 120 BPM synthwave musical soundtrack loop are synthesized procedurally in Web Audio Float32Array PCM buffers on startup, requiring zero external audio file downloads.
  • On-Device Facial AI Inference: Powered by Google MediaPipe Tasks Vision WebAssembly, webcam inference runs asynchronously at 20–30 Hz decoupled from the 60 FPS rendering loop. Adaptive One-Euro filtering, deadzone compensation, and expression hysteresis deliver responsive, jitter-free facial steering and morphing.
  • 100% Privacy-First Processing: All webcam video frames are processed exclusively in client-side RAM inside WebAssembly memory. Video data is never recorded, never saved to disk, and never transmitted over any network.
